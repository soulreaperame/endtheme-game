# EndTheme

**Engine:** Unreal Engine 5.5
**Platform:** Windows PC
**Project:** UNIMAS Final Year Project

---

## Overview
**EndTheme** is a 3D narrative adventure game exploring air pollution through atmospheric, character-driven storytelling rather than charts and statistics — aiming to build emotional engagement and environmental awareness through exploration and world design.

---

## Features
- **Metaphorical Narrative** — a linear story highlighting the human impact of air pollution, favoring emotional connection over data
- **Atmospheric Visuals** — procedural terrain (Gaea), volumetric fog, and smog-filled environments
- **Exploration & Dialogue** — third-person adventure with NPC interactions and minimal combat
- **Dynamic Environment** — the world's color palette shifts from muted gray to richer tones as the story progresses
